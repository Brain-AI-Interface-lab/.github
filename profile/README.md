## Brain-AI Interface (BAI) lab

Brain-AI Interface (BAI) lab is dedicated to decoding the complex information-processing principles of the brain and translating these discoveries into next-generation brain-computer interfaces (BCI) and artificial intelligence technologies. By integrating foundational neuroscience with engineering modeling, we aim to develop practical technologies that bridge the gap between humans and machines, ultimately improving the quality of human life.

### Vision
The human and primate brain possesses an astonishing ability to continuously predict sensory information and precisely correct motor commands, even in highly uncertain environments. Our research seeks to uncover the neural mechanisms underlying this "sensory prediction and motor correction" from a mathematical and computational perspective. We believe that understanding the superiority of the biological brain is a crucial key to overcoming the limitations of current AI and designing better, more adaptive systems.
